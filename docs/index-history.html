The Decay Index is the share of textbook-registry rows with at least 10 out-of-sample trades that are currently badged DEAD (dead ÷ (alive + fading + dead)). It only ever grows week by week, as this pipeline runs and archives another snapshot to results/history/<as_of>.json — nobody, including us, can go back and add last year's snapshot after the fact. With only a week or two of history the chart below will be short; that is expected, not a bug, and its value compounds precisely because it can't be reconstructed retroactively.
